At Code Vedic IT Solutions we believe in transparency and fairness. This Refund Policy explains the conditions under which refunds may be issued for our website development, mobile app development, SEO/SMO, and IT training services.

1. Overview

Because digital services involve significant time, customization, and resources from the moment work begins, refunds are handled on a case-by-case basis according to the stage of the project and the reason for the request. All refund requests must be submitted in writing to info@codevedic.in.

2. Website & Mobile App Development Projects

  • Before work starts: If you cancel after paying an advance but before any work has begun (no design, no development, no resource allocation), you may be eligible for a full refund of the advance, minus any non-refundable third-party costs already incurred (e.g., domain, hosting setup if paid on your behalf).
  • After discovery / planning phase: Once requirements gathering, wireframes, or detailed planning have started, a partial refund may be considered after deducting the value of work already completed (typically 20–40% of the advance).
  • After design or development has begun: Once design mockups or coding has started, refunds are generally not available for the portion of work already delivered. Any remaining unused advance may be refunded at our discretion after deducting completed work and committed costs.
  • After final delivery & acceptance: Once the project is delivered and accepted (or deemed accepted after the review period), no refunds will be issued.

Milestone-based payments: Each milestone payment is considered earned upon completion and approval of that milestone and is non-refundable.

3. SEO & Social Media Optimization (SMO) Services

  • SEO and SMO are ongoing services. Fees paid for a completed month or package period are non-refundable.
  • If you cancel mid-cycle, any unused portion of a prepaid package may be considered for a pro-rata refund at our discretion, after deducting work already performed and setup costs.
  • We do not guarantee specific rankings, traffic numbers, or conversions. Lack of expected results alone does not entitle a refund, as search and social algorithms are outside our complete control.

4. IT Training Programs

  • Before course start: Full refund (minus any non-refundable registration or material fees, if applicable) if cancellation is requested at least 7 days before the course start date.
  • Within first 3 days / first 2 sessions: Partial refund (typically 50–70%) may be available if you are not satisfied and notify us in writing, provided materials have not been fully accessed.
  • After significant course progress: No refund once more than 25% of the course content or sessions have been completed or materials accessed.
  • Recorded courses or self-paced materials: Once access is granted, refunds are generally not available.

5. Cancellation by the Client

You may cancel a project or service at any time by written notice. Refund eligibility will be determined according to the sections above based on the stage of work and amounts already paid. Any work product completed up to the cancellation date remains our property until full payment for that work is settled, or as otherwise agreed.

6. Cancellation by Code Vedic IT Solutions

We reserve the right to cancel or suspend a project if:

  • Payments are not received according to the agreed schedule
  • The client fails to provide required materials or feedback within a reasonable time
  • The project involves illegal, unethical, or prohibited content
  • Circumstances beyond our reasonable control make completion impossible

In such cases, we will refund any unearned portion of fees after deducting work already performed and costs incurred.

7. How to Request a Refund

  1. Email info@codevedic.in with the subject line “Refund Request – [Your Name / Project Name]”.
  2. Include: full name, contact details, project or course name, payment date/amount, and reason for the request.
  3. We will acknowledge receipt within 2–3 business days and review the request.
  4. Approved refunds will be processed within 7–14 business days to the original payment method (or bank transfer if original method is unavailable).

8. Non-Refundable Items

The following are generally non-refundable:

  • Third-party costs already paid on your behalf (domain registration, hosting, premium plugins, app store fees, stock assets, advertising spend, etc.)
  • Completed and approved milestones
  • Services already delivered and accepted
  • Custom design or development work that has been started and shared with you
  • Any fees marked as non-refundable in a specific proposal or invoice

9. Payment Terms

Payment schedules (advance, milestones, final payment) are detailed in individual project proposals. Late payments may result in suspension of work. All prices are exclusive of applicable taxes unless stated otherwise. This Refund Policy should be read together with our Terms & Conditions.
