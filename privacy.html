Code Vedic IT Solutions (“we”, “our”, or “us”) respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you share with us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit www.codevedic.in or use our services.

1. Introduction

This policy applies to information collected through our website, contact forms, enquiry pop-ups, chat widget, WhatsApp communications, and any related services. By using our website or providing us with your information, you consent to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information you provide voluntarily

  • Contact & enquiry data: Name, email address, phone number, service of interest, and message content submitted via contact form, enquiry popup, or chat widget.
  • Project-related data: Business details, requirements, branding assets, and other information necessary to deliver website, app, SEO, or training services.
  • Training registration: Name, contact details, and course preferences when you enrol in IT training programs.

2.2 Information collected automatically

  • IP address, browser type, device type, operating system
  • Pages visited, time spent, referring URL
  • Approximate location derived from IP (city/region level)

We do not intentionally collect sensitive personal data (such as Aadhaar, financial passwords, or health information) unless required for a specific service and with your explicit consent.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to your enquiries and provide the services you request
  • Prepare proposals, quotes, and project plans
  • Deliver website, app, SEO/SMO, and training services
  • Send project updates, invoices, and support communications
  • Improve our website, services, and user experience
  • Comply with legal obligations and protect our rights
  • Send occasional promotional or educational emails (you may opt out at any time)

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We may share data only in the following circumstances:

  • Service providers: Trusted third parties who help us operate (e.g., form processing via FormSubmit, hosting, email delivery). They are bound by confidentiality obligations.
  • Legal requirements: When required by law, court order, or government request, or to protect our rights, safety, or property.
  • Business transfer: In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction.

5. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

Our website may use essential cookies and similar technologies to ensure proper functioning (e.g., remembering form state or localStorage for the enquiry popup). We currently do not use third-party advertising or analytics cookies that track you across sites. If we introduce analytics tools in the future, we will update this policy and, where required, obtain consent.

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect website functionality.

6. Data Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. These include secure form handling, HTTPS, and limited access to data. However, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes for which it was collected, including providing services, complying with legal obligations, resolving disputes, and enforcing agreements. Enquiry and contact data are typically retained for up to 24 months unless a longer period is required for an active project or legal reason. You may request deletion earlier (see Your Rights).

8. Your Rights

Depending on applicable law (including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 in India), you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Request deletion of your data (subject to legal exceptions)
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent
  • Object to or restrict certain processing

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at info@codevedic.in. We will respond within a reasonable timeframe.

9. Children’s Privacy

Our website and services are not directed at children under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will take steps to delete it.

10. Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to external sites (social media, WhatsApp, etc.). We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those third-party websites. We encourage you to read their privacy policies.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date will be revised when changes are made. We encourage you to review this page periodically. Continued use of the website after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
