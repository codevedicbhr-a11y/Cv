5 Simple SEO Tips to Rank Higher on Google

Search Engine Optimisation does not have to be complicated. Start with these five practical steps:

  1. Use clear page titles and headings that include the words people actually search for.
  2. Write helpful content that answers real questions instead of stuffing keywords.
  3. Make your site mobile-friendly and fast — Google rewards sites that load quickly on phones.
  4. Add your business to Google Business Profile so local customers can find you on Maps.
  5. Get a few quality backlinks from local directories, partners or news sites.

Flutter vs Native: Which Should You Choose for Your App?

Native apps (Swift for iOS, Kotlin for Android) give the best performance and deepest device integration, but you need two separate codebases — higher cost and longer timelines.

Flutter lets you build one app that runs on both Android and iOS from a single codebase. It is fast to develop, looks modern, and is excellent for most business apps, marketplaces and internal tools.

For the majority of startups and SMEs, Flutter offers the best balance of speed, cost and quality. We specialise in Flutter development at Code Vedic and can guide you based on your exact requirements.
