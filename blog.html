5 Simple SEO Tips for Local Businesses in Bihar

You don’t need a big budget to start ranking on Google. These five practical tips help local businesses get found by the right customers.

  1. Claim and complete your Google Business Profile — photos, hours, services and reviews matter a lot for local search.
  2. Use location keywords — include your city and area naturally in titles and content (e.g. “Website development in Patna”).
  3. Make your site mobile-friendly and fast — Google rewards sites that load quickly on phones.
  4. Add a clear call-to-action — phone number, WhatsApp button and contact form should be easy to find.
  5. Publish helpful content — short blogs or service pages that answer real questions your customers ask.
